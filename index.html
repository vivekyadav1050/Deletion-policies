Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Welcome to Aerio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our mobile application (com.healthlive.app) and connected AP-20 Bluetooth health monitoring devices.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate health tracking and synchronization, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and authentication credentials provided during sign-in.
  • Health Measurements: SpO2 (Blood Oxygen), Pulse Rate (PR), Perfusion Index (PI), and timestamps collected from compatible Bluetooth medical devices (AP-20).
  • Device Permissions: Bluetooth and location permissions strictly required to discover, pair, and communicate with your health monitor.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To display real-time health metrics on your app dashboard.
  • To securely upload and store measurement history for patient monitoring.
  • To maintain app security, authentication, and troubleshoot connectivity issues.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard security practices, including HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the app and our backend servers, ensuring your health records remain secure.

Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

How to request permanent deletion of your account and data

At Aerio, you maintain full ownership and control over your personal and health records. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can request it easily.

How to Request Deletion:

  1. Send an Email: Email our support team directly at viveksinghald1050@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Email Subject: Use the subject line: Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Required Information: Include your registered username and Patient ID in the message body so we can verify and locate your records.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your account credentials and profile information.
  • All stored SpO2, Pulse Rate, and PI measurement history.
  • Associated patient and tablet logs.

Processing Timeline

Upon receiving your deletion request, our team will process and permanently erase all your data from our active databases within 14 business days. A confirmation email will be sent once completed.

Support Contact: For any assistance regarding account removal, reach out to us at viveksinghald1050@gmail.com.
