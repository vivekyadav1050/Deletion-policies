Data Deletion Instructions

Request deletion of your Aerio account and associated health records

At Aerio, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal and health data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated measurement records, you can follow the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion:

  1. Via Email: Send an email to our support team at viveksinghald1050@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Email Subject: Please use the subject line: Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Required Details: In the body of the email, include your registered username/email and Patient ID so we can locate and delete your data securely.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Your account credentials and profile information.
  • All synced SpO2, Pulse Rate, and PI health measurement history.
  • Associated patient and tablet association logs.

Processing Time

Once your request is received via email, our team will verify and permanently delete all your data from our servers within 14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

Need Help? If you have any questions regarding data removal, contact us directly at viveksinghald1050@gmail.com.
